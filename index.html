What PayCheckpoint can — and cannot — do

✓ It can

  • ✓ Compare intended, instructed and receipt numbers & amounts digit-by-digit
  • ✓ Parse a pasted SMS into editable fields — missing fields stay missing
  • ✓ Build a private offline action plan and optionally request a redacted AI plan
  • ✓ Organise evidence, timeline and a factual complaint draft you copy yourself
  • ✓ Review labelled multi-message conversations for ordered warning patterns locally — not a fraud verdict

✕ It cannot

  • ✕ Intercept, cancel, reverse or freeze a real MFS transfer or wallet
  • ✕ Verify who owns a number without authorised provider data
  • ✕ Guarantee a refund or submit a complaint on your behalf
  • ✕ Act without provider cooperation & regulatory approval — none is claimed

Safety & privacy

Payment-message parsing and screenshot comparison happen locally. The optional Copilot can send a best-effort redacted summary and coarse categories to Cloudflare Workers AI only after explicit consent. Redaction cannot guarantee anonymity, so do not paste raw SMS or include names, phone/account details, payment references, PINs or OTPs. Cloudflare documents zero retention for standard stateless Workers AI inference after processing; operational metadata may be retained. The free daily allocation is limited and may be exhausted. Never share a PIN or OTP with anyone.

Frequently asked questions

Can PayCheckpoint cancel my bKash / Nagad / Rocket payment?

No. Only the provider can act on a real transfer under its own policies. PayCheckpoint is a prototype companion; it cannot access your wallet or contact the provider. Its recovery examples use fictional data. The Undo demonstration is a simulation only.

Will I get my money back?

PayCheckpoint cannot return money or guarantee recovery. Contact your provider through its official channel promptly and follow its current process. Outcomes depend on the provider and the circumstances. Never pay a recovery fee or share a PIN or OTP.

Are you integrated with bKash, Nagad or Rocket?

No integration is claimed. The Recovery Assistant now links to official provider and regulator contact pages. Contact details were checked on 28 October 2026, but can change; verify the current details at the source before acting. PayCheckpoint does not place calls or submit complaints.

Is my transaction data stored?

Case details are kept in this page’s memory by default and are not saved to a PayCheckpoint account. On a hosted build, the optional Copilot can send a best-effort-redacted summary and broad categories to Cloudflare Workers AI only after your consent. Redaction is not guaranteed anonymization. Do not include identifying details or secrets. Clear page data in Privacy & Settings.

Why does it say “recipient not verified”?

Because it isn't. Without authorised provider data, showing a name as “verified” would be misleading. The comparison uses numbers and amounts only.

Design system, flows & developer handoff


UX brief: Audience — everyday MFS users in Bangladesh, mixed digital literacy, often stressed. Problem — one wrong digit is irreversible-feeling and recovery steps are fragmented. Principles: compare exactly, label provenance, never over-claim, Bangla-first, calm under stress. IA: Home → Start → {Prevent: Intent → SMS → Reconciliation → SafeSend → Pending → Result} / {Recover: SMS → Reconciliation → Guidance → Incident} → Privacy & Settings.

Type & layout
Inter (Latin) + Noto Sans Bengali · Scale 12/13/14/15/17/20/24/32/42 · Spacing 4-pt (4–48) · Radius 10/14/16/999 · Breakpoints: mobile 360–430 (priority), tablet ~768, desktop ~1180 max-width · Touch targets ≥44px · Focus: 3px amber outline · Status never by colour alone — always icon + label.


Components & states
Buttons (primary / outline / amber / danger / ghost × default·hover·focus·pressed·disabled·loading), inputs + numeric inputs with associated error text, step indicator, provenance tags (Extracted from pasted SMS Reported by user Derived by comparison Present in original instruction Unknown / unverified), comparison table, countdown, dialogs, warning / error / success panels, timeline, copy & download controls, skeleton/loading on SMS parse.


Edge-state inventory
Empty form · invalid number (not 11 digits / wrong prefix) · missing amount · missing TrxID (Scenario E) · unsupported SMS · parse failure with manual entry · instruction conflict · countdown expiry · undo success / repeated undo / double-click guard · navigate-away during pending · undo after completion (button disabled, routes to Recovery) · generic error result state.


Accessibility review (target WCAG 2.2 AA)
Labels on every field, errors linked via aria-describedby, aria-live on countdown & parse status, visible focus, ≥44px targets, reduced-motion respected, numbers in tabular-friendly formatting, Bangla text expansion allowed (no fixed-height text containers).


Brand
Wordmark: “PayCheckpoint” in Inter 800 with a teal rounded-square “P” mark and amber checkpoint dot — the dot = the pause point before money moves. Not a shield, not a currency symbol. Works on ivory and charcoal backgrounds; legible at 16px.


Assumptions (documented, per brief)
30-second undo window is illustrative; recovery contacts are source-linked and were checked on 28 October 2026 but must be re-verified before use; all sample transaction data in this prototype is synthetic.
